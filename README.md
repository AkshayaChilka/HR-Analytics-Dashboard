# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary trends, and employee engagement using Power BI.

The dashboard provides interactive insights into employee turnover, department-wise attrition, salary distribution, overtime impact, and workforce analytics to support data-driven HR decision-making.

---

## Tools & Technologies Used
- Excel
- Power BI
- DAX
- Data Cleaning
- Data Visualization

---

## Dashboard Features
- Employee Attrition Analysis
- Department-wise Attrition Insights
- Salary Trend Analysis
- Gender Distribution Analysis
- Workforce KPI Tracking
- Interactive Slicers & Filters
- Business Insights & Recommendations

---

## Key KPIs
- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Attrition Rate (%)
- Average Monthly Salary
- Average Employee Age
- Average Years at Company

---

## Key Workforce Insights
- Research & Development department shows the highest employee attrition.
- Employees working overtime are more likely to leave the organization.
- Male employees constitute the majority of the workforce.
- Managers and Research Directors receive the highest average salaries.
- Employees with lower job satisfaction exhibit higher attrition trends.
- Current attrition patterns highlight employee retention challenges.

## Project Objectives

## Dataset Information
Dataset used: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset

The dataset contains employee-related information such as:
- Age
- Gender
- Department
- Job Role
- Monthly Income
- Job Satisfaction
- Attrition Status
- Overtime
- Years at Company
